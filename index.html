How many charter schools are there in New York City?

287 schools operating on 415 campuses across the five boroughs.

How these numbers were verified · Full validation report

What we counted

A charter school is an independent public school operating under a charter granted under Article 56 of the New York State Education Law, authorized by the SUNY Trustees, the NYS Board of Regents or the NYC Schools Chancellor. We count schools open with students in 2026–27 with a campus in the five boroughs. A school is one legal charter; a campus is that school operating in a building. Schools approved but not yet open, closed or merged are not counted, and are listed below with the reason.

    How the count was verified

    1. Legal list first

      The 287 schools come from the authorizers: SUNY's 2026–27 school list, the Board of Regents roster adjusted for every Regents action through May 2026, and the state directory for Chancellor schools. One operating school missing from SUNY's list, renewed through 2029, was added back.

    2. Two sources per campus

      Every campus is confirmed by at least two independent sources: the authorizer's site list, the NYC Charter School Center finder, the city's 2025–26 site list, the school's website, or dated 2026 documents.

    3. Every location checked

      All addresses were placed with the city's geocoder and compared across sources. Three geocoder errors and four source errors were found and corrected.

    4. Counts reconciled

      All 420 listings in the Charter Center's finder are accounted for one by one: a campus here, a second listing for the same campus, an old address, or a test record.

    5. Blind audit

      Thirty randomly drawn schools were rechecked by an independent pass that never saw this dataset. All 39 addresses it found are here, with no contradictions.

    Every source, checksum and judgment call is in the validation report.
